# Proposals

The Proposals Dashboard provides an intuitive overview of all active and past proposals for any DAO within the Governance.so platform. It’s designed to help users quickly assess DAO activity and easily participate in governance decisions.

## 📊 Key Metrics at a Glance

At the top of the page, you’ll find summary metrics that reflect the DAO’s overall governance activity, including:

* Total Votes Cast
* Proposal Success Rate
* Counts of Passed and Defeated Proposals

These metrics give you a quick snapshot of the DAO’s health and engagement.

## 🗳️ Browsing Proposals

Below the metrics, you’ll see a searchable list of proposals. The search feature scans both proposal titles and descriptions to help you locate relevant items quickly.

* Active proposals are highlighted in white text
* Completed proposals (passed or defeated) are shown in grey

Clicking on any proposal opens the [Proposal Details Prompt](proposal-details.md), where you can:

* View full proposal details
* Review vote history and status
* Cast your vote or take relevant action (when active)
