# Proposal Details

Each DAO on Governance.so has a dedicated page, accessible via a unique address (e.g., [Grape DAO](https://governance.so/dao/grape)). From any DAO page, proposals are displayed in a list—active proposals are shown in white, while completed proposals are greyed out.

Clicking on a proposal opens the Proposal Prompt, a detailed view that provides everything you need to understand and participate in the governance process.

## 🔍 What’s Inside the Proposal Prompt

*   Proposal Title & Description

    Includes parsed and rendered content from Gist links (formatted as HTML when applicable) for rich and informative descriptions.
*   Author & Proposal Type

    Displays the proposer’s name and the type of proposal (e.g., _Community_, _Council_).
* Voting Panel (Right Sidebar)
  * Live voting results (for/against/abstain)
  * Voting buttons (if the proposal is active) -  (see [voting on a proposal](voting-on-a-proposal.md))
  * Expandable view for proposal rules and additional parameters
  * Voting power shown when your wallet is connected
*   Voter Breakdown

    A detailed list of all participants who voted, along with their voting power or weight.
