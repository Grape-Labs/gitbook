# About

Governance.so was developed to deliver a modern, user-friendly, and powerful interface for DAO governance on Solana. It enhances the standard governance experience by introducing advanced tools, intuitive design, and a focus on usability—whether you’re a new DAO participant or a seasoned contributor.

## 🧩 The Problem

DAO governance can be complex and unintuitive—especially for newcomers. Even experienced members often find themselves repeating manual tasks or navigating inefficient interfaces. These challenges highlight a clear need for a more streamlined, intelligent solution.

## ✅ The Need & Our Solution

The Grape DAO needed a governance interface that reduced friction, enhanced functionality, and offered a more intuitive user experience.

Governance.so meets this need by providing a full-featured, accessible platform designed to:

* Simplify proposal creation and participation
* Integrate seamlessly with existing Solana wallets
* Enhance transparency and decision-making through data-rich insights

## 🚀 Key Features

* User-Centered Design – clean, responsive, and mobile-friendly interface
* Customizable Proposal Templates – streamline recurring proposals with saved formats
* Advanced Voting Mechanisms – enhanced clarity and participation tools
* Treasury Management – integrated tools to visualize and manage DAO funds
* DAO Filtering – users can easily view DAOs where they hold voting power
* Analytics & Metrics – real-time data to support informed decision-making
* Extensibility – support for governance program extensions and future upgrades
